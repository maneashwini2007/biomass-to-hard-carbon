# Biomass to Hard Carbon for Sodium-Ion Batteries

**An agricultural waste valorization project for sustainable energy storage.**

## Problem Statement

The growing demand for battery energy storage increases the need for sustainable and locally available anode materials. Conventional hard carbon production and dependence on imported materials can create supply-chain and cost challenges.

## Our Solution

We aim to convert locally available agricultural biomass into hard carbon for potential use as an anode material in sodium-ion batteries.

## Objectives

* Identify suitable agricultural biomass feedstocks.
* Study and compare biomass-to-hard-carbon production methods.
* Evaluate production costs and raw material availability.
* Develop a laboratory-scale prototype and characterize the resulting carbon.

## Target Users

* Sodium-ion battery manufacturers
* Battery material research laboratories
* Energy storage startups
* Agricultural waste processing industries

## Project Workflow

1. Biomass collection and pretreatment
2. Carbonization and thermal processing
3. Material characterization
4. Electrochemical performance evaluation
5. Cost and scalability assessment

## Current Status

Ideathon research and feasibility analysis.

## Future Scope

Laboratory validation, prototype development, and evaluation of hard carbon as a sodium-ion battery anode.
